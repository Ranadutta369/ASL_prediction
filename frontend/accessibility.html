Assistive Technology & Accessibility Guide

Controls and learning guidance for speech-impaired learners, educators, children, and users with different visual or motor needs.

Accessibility Preferences

High Contrast Mode Improves text visibility and hand landmark contrast.
Dyslexia-Friendly Font Switches the interface to a simpler fallback font stack.
Large Touch Targets Increases button size for touch, children, and motor accessibility.

For Speech-Impaired Users

  • Real-time voice output: Converts stable recognized letters into spoken audio.
  • Sentence builder: Builds words and sentences from recognized signs.
  • Text-to-sign player: Converts selected prompts into 3D hand landmark animations.

For Learners and Educators

  • Visual feedback: Shows hand joints and connections over the camera feed.
  • ASL dictionary: Provides simple instructions for letters, phrases, and numbers.
  • Top candidates: Shows alternatives when a hand shape is partially formed.

AI System Overview

  • Normalized landmarks: Hand points are centered on the wrist and scale-normalized.
  • Letter classifier: The backend predicts A-Z letters from 63 landmark values.
  • Sign synthesizer: The generator renders precomputed 3D landmark trajectories.
